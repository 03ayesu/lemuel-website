    About me

    My first computer was in a school ICT lab in Ghana. I was so little I can't remember what I did on it, but I was hooked. I kept talking about computers until my dad bought me a PC. I opened it up, broke things, replaced motherboards, and asked my ICT teacher a lot of questions.

    All my plans were IT. Then I learned design for my final year project, and my first job after school was a design job. I found out I could really do it. When an IT job came up, I took it, because I can do design from anywhere but IT not so much. Now I do both, and I'm not giving up either.

    Running real systems changed how I design. I use software and wonder if the person who designed it ever knew how it would be used. That's why I start with the people who'll use it.

    I enjoy designing mobile apps most, because every app has its own flow and style. When something breaks, I like the problems that make me feel I don't know much, because that means I have something new to learn. I don't believe in mediocrity, and I want to be the best at everything I touch.

    The project I'm proud of

    Driver and Vehicle Licensing Authority

    I worked on a platform built end to end, with a website for citizens, a system for businesses to manage their fleets, and tools for the authority to manage its clients and site visits. It was the first time I knew my work could reach a lot of people, and it was hard because the early documentation wasn't clear.
